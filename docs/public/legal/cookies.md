# Cookies and storage

Effective 23 October 2026.

Latte sets no cookies, and there are no advertising or cross-site tracking tools on the site.

It keeps a few things in your browser's own storage, only to make the app work:

| What | Where | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your sign-in session | local storage (`sb-…-auth-token`) | to keep you signed in | until you sign out, or the session expires |
| Your colour theme | local storage | to remember light, dark or system | until you clear it |
| The call you are playing | session storage | to keep playing across pages | until you close the tab |

Page views are counted by Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and stores no personal identifiers.

You can clear this storage in your browser at any time; you will be signed out.
